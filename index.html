    How to play

    You have 60 seconds. Name as many things in the category as you can.

    • Every answer is worth 0 to 1000 points, based on how rare it is.
    • Common picks score a little. Deep cuts score big.
    • At the end you get bonus points for your strategy: go wide with lots of answers, or go deep with only rare ones. Both can win.
